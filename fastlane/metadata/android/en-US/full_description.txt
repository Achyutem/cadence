Cadence is a personal daily operating system: tasks, habits, a calendar, notes, reminders,
breath training and home screen widgets, in one app that never touches the network.

It declares no INTERNET permission. That is the guarantee everything else rests on. There is
no account, no backend, no sync, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your data
lives in a local database on your device, and the only way it leaves is a JSON file you
export yourself.

FEATURES

Today: one screen for what is due, what you are maintaining and what is next.

Tasks: subtasks, priorities, scheduling, recurrence, and a backlog for anything without a date.

Habits: four measurement types (done, count, quantity, duration), goals in either direction,
streaks, consistency and a contribution heatmap.

Calendar: month, week and day views over the same data.

Notes: Markdown, autosaved, with checkboxes you can tick in the rendered view.

Reminders: local notifications with actions that work without opening the app.

Breath training: box breathing, static apnea, CO2 and O2 tables, every one configurable in
rounds, hold, rest and increment, with optional audio cues so you can keep your eyes closed.

Widgets: seven interactive home screen widgets. Tick a task or step a habit from the home
screen without launching the app.

Insights: completion rates, patterns and trends, computed on device from your own rows.

NO GAMIFICATION

No XP, no levels, no badges, no points, no confetti, no streak rewards. A streak is
information, never a prize and never a threat. An empty day is not a failed day, and nothing
in the app is written to make you feel otherwise.

DESIGN

Material 3 with light and dark themes, ten accent colours and Material You. Geist Variable is
the only bundled asset. Beyond Jetpack and kotlinx there are no third party libraries: the
Markdown parser, the heatmap and the charts are all written for this app rather than pulled
in as dependencies.
